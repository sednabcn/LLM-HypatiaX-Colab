Core AMM Formulas

This is the most widely used formula, where the quantities of two tokens multiplied together equals a constant value Collective ShiftFaisal Khan. Used by protocols like Uniswap and SushiSwap.
2. Constant Sum Formula (x + y = k)
This ensures the total sum of tokens in the pool remains constant Faisal Khan. More suitable for stablecoin pairs but can create arbitrage opportunities.
3. Constant Mean Market Maker (CMMM)
This extends the constant product model to accommodate multiple assets while maintaining a constant mean price, such as (xyz)^(1/3) = k for three assets Cyfrin. Used by Balancer.
4. Hybrid Models
These combine aspects of different formulas to reduce issues like impermanent loss or slippage Faisal Khan. Curve Finance pioneered this for stablecoin trading.
5. Dynamic Market Makers
These adjust pricing formulas based on market conditions, trading volume, or other factors to provide more responsive trading Cyfrin.
6. Concentrated Liquidity
Introduced by Uniswap v3, this represents an evolution in how liquidity can be provided rather than a completely different formula.
