Strategy 2 (Joint Training) - Simple

Key Advantages of Joint Training

Simpler data requirements: Just need (description, formula) pairs - no entity annotations needed
No error propagation: Errors don't cascade through multiple steps
Easier to train: One model vs. 4 separate models
More realistic: Mimics how you'd actually use the system
