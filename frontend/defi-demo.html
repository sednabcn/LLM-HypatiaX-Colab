ℹ️ What is Impermanent Loss?

Impermanent Loss (IL) is the difference between holding tokens in a liquidity pool versus holding them in your wallet.

  • 0% to -1%: Minimal loss - generally acceptable
  • -1% to -5%: Moderate loss - monitor carefully
  • -5% to -10%: Significant loss - fees should compensate
  • Below -10%: Severe loss - consider exiting unless high APY

Note: IL can be offset by trading fees earned from the pool. Use the Complete Position Analysis below for a full picture.

ℹ️ Understanding the Metrics

Key Metrics Explained:

  • Net P&L: Total profit/loss including fees and IL
  • Fee Income: Trading fees earned from the pool
  • HODL Value: What your tokens would be worth if you just held them
  • Pool Quality Score: 0-100 rating based on volume and TVL
  • Daily Turnover: Daily volume as % of TVL (higher = more fees)
  • Days to Break Even: How long until fees offset IL

Quality Score Ranges:

  • 80-100: Excellent - High volume, good for LPs
  • 60-79: Good - Decent fee generation
  • 40-59: Average - Monitor performance
  • Below 40: Poor - Consider alternatives
