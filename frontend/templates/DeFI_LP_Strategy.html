📌 Framework: Fee tier selection depends on volatility. Lower volatility = tighter fee tier possible. Higher volatility = higher fee tier needed to offset IL.

💡 Key Insights

  • • Stablecoins (0% vol): Use 0.01-0.05% tiers for maximum fees with zero IL
  • • Low vol (0.5-3%): 0.05% tier works; tight ranges maximize APY
  • • Medium vol (3-10%): 0.30% becomes essential; cap APY gains but protects capital
  • • High vol (10%+): 1.00% tier or avoid; fees rarely cover IL

⚡ Capital Allocation

  • • Ultra-low vol: 100% capital in tight ranges
  • • Low vol: 90% capital near price, 10% reserved
  • • Medium vol: 80/20 split across wider ranges
  • • High vol: 50/50 split, active rebalancing required
